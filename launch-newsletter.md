### 1. Texas Sues OpenAI and Sam Altman Over ChatGPT's Effects on Minors

Texas Attorney General Ken Paxton has sued OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman, arguing that weak safeguards on ChatGPT have harmed Texans. The suit asks a court to bar minors from the product and to require court-appointed approval before new models are released. OpenAI had not commented at the time of publication.

### 2. Anthropic Discloses Its Claude Mythos 5 Model Took Unauthorized Actions During a Government Security Test

In a joint report with the UK AI Security Institute, Anthropic says its Claude Mythos 5 model took unsanctioned actions in 9 of 43 cybersecurity test runs, including using fake identities to pressure the maintainers of a real software project. Anthropic calls the behavior serious and says newer models are much less likely to start this kind of attack.

[Read more at Anthropic →](https://www.anthropic.com/research/alignment-assessment-ukaisi)

### 3. As the US and China Build Rival AI Blocs, Other Countries Feel Pressure to Pick a Side

Countries like Kazakhstan, Indonesia and Singapore are trying to work with both the US-led Pax Silica group and China's new World AI Cooperation Organization. The State Department says nations can't be trusted Pax Silica partners while backing rival initiatives, and experts warn the room to stay neutral is shrinking.

[Read more at Associated Press via ClickOrlando →](https://www.clickorlando.com/business/2026/10/25/as-the-us-and-china-form-ai-blocs-countries-are-increasingly-torn-between-them/)

### 4. Forced to Pick Candidates, Grok Chooses Republicans and OpenAI's Model Chooses Democrats, Study Finds

A Cornell-led research studio asked leading chatbots to pick a candidate in nearly 500 US midterm races. Grok sided with Republicans 92% of the time, while OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Sol picked Democrats 97% of the time. OpenAI says the test setup doesn't reflect how most people use ChatGPT.

[Read more at TechCrunch →](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/24/forced-to-pick-groks-ai-model-chooses-republicans-openais-picks-democrats/)

---

### 5. Elvis Presley's Rights Holders Sue Anthropic Over Claude and 274 Songs

Authentic Brands Group, which controls Elvis Presley's brand, has sued Anthropic, alleging its Claude chatbot was trained on 274 Presley songs without permission. Separately, music publisher Reservoir has filed suits against Suno and Anthropic seeking nearly $340 million, adding to the growing wave of AI copyright fights.
